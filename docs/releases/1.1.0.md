Parental controls, and the Android app brought level with the iPhone app.

- **Parental controls.** Turn them on in the dashboard (People & devices). Every adult profile then needs a PIN, only the admin can add profiles, and each kids profile gets an age limit: all ages, 7+, 10+, 13+, 16+ or 18+. Anything above the limit does not appear for that profile, in rows, search, title pages, notifications or downloads. Titles with no age rating are hidden from kids too.
- **Lowering a limit keeps the watchlist.** Titles now above it stay on a kids profile's watchlist, dimmed with a padlock, and cannot be opened or played.
- **Android plays inside the title page.** Play turns the title's artwork into the player, with the episodes and details still underneath. Turn the phone sideways, or tap Full Screen, for the whole screen; turn it back upright and it carries on in the page.
- **A new Android player.** Settings beside the picture, playback stats, six speeds, pinch to fill, thumbnails while scrubbing, a slide to unlock, episodes beside the picture with downloads, and Picture in Picture in the film's own shape. Playback starts at the resume point without a jump from the beginning.
- **Android downloads** list what was asked for (a film, an episode, a season), each with an inspector showing pieces, files, peers and trackers, and a way to remove the torrent's copy once seeding is done.
- **Android, everywhere else.** Failures are a short message at the top of the screen instead of raw text, pull down to refresh Home, the Library and the Watchlist, swipe a title off the Watchlist, and a redesigned title page with its button on the artwork and tabs that stay in place.
- **Romanian age ratings** (AG, AP and the numbered ones) are understood.
- **Updates carry on** when the qBittorrent image cannot be downloaded but the same version is already on the server.
